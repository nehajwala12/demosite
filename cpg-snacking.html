The governing finding

Marketed leverage of 4.3× holds only on management EBITDA of $42.0MM. On the EBITDA a secured lender can actually attach — $31.2MM — the same $180MM facility is 5.8×. Every covenant, every case, and the price of this deal moves with that one choice.

CreditSwan does not tell the committee which denominator to use. It shows all three, reconciles the walk between them line-by-line, and flags where the bridge depends on synergies, unowned periods, or add-backs that recur. The committee decides — on evidence, not on a teaser.

Contribution to attributable EBITDA vs. share of the risk

The evidence in four quadrants
What supports the credit
  • Category growing 7% p.a.; Northwind gaining 180bps of share over 24 months on velocity, not just distribution
  • 38.2% gross margin, +240bps YoY — pricing has stuck through two list increases with no volume loss
  • Positive FCF in each of the last 6 quarters; low maintenance capex (1.4% of sales)
  • Founder-CEO rolling 40% of equity — meaningful alignment below the debt
What pressures the credit
  • $10.8MM of the $42.0MM EBITDA is add-backs; $6.2MM does not clear CreditSwan's recurrence test
  • Top-3 customers = 47% of revenue; largest reauthorizes Q1 each year
  • Base case assumes $3.1MM of synergies not yet in run-rate financials
  • Two input costs (oats, packaging film) are 100% single-sourced
What the data room does not resolve
  • Trade-spend accruals reconcile to the TB but not to the customer deduction detail — $1.2MM unexplained
  • The acquired co-man line has 4 months of unowned EBITDA in LTM
  • No third-party confirmation of the club reauthorization beyond a verbal from management
  • Stock-comp normalization applied inconsistently across the two adjustment schedules
What external evidence says
  • Retail scanner data (52-wk) corroborates the +11% organic volume claim within 60bps
  • Job-posting and review signal shows the club relationship active and expanding SKUs as of Jul-26
  • Two named competitors cut list prices in Q2 — a margin watch item, not yet a breach risk
  • No litigation, lien, or UCC red flags in the external sweep (26 Jul)
