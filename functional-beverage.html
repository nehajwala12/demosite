The governing finding

Marketed leverage of 4.4× rests on management EBITDA of $32.0MM. On the EBITDA a secured lender can attach — $24.0MM — the same $140MM facility is 5.8×. And the growth that supports it is distribution, not velocity: strip the one-time club load-in and organic sell-through is +9%, not +31%.

CreditSwan reconciles shipment revenue to third-party sell-through, isolates load-in from repeat velocity, and shows all three leverage bases so the committee decides on evidence, not on a teaser.

The evidence in four quadrants
What supports the credit
  • Category growing 6% p.a.; brand gaining share on a differentiated electrolyte format
  • 41% gross margin, +180bps YoY; one list increase held with no volume loss
  • DTC/Amazon 26% of revenue and the highest-margin channel, growing 41%
  • Founder-CEO rolling 38% of equity below the debt
What pressures the credit
  • Growth is distribution-led; velocity per point is flat
  • Top customer (national club) is 26% of revenue; reauthorizes annually
  • Base case assumes $2.6MM of synergies not yet in run-rate financials
  • Aluminum cans and a flavor concentrate are single-sourced
What the data room does not resolve
  • Club load-in vs. sell-through split relies on management allocation
  • Trade accrual reconciles to the TB but not to deduction detail (~$0.9MM)
  • Acquired distributor has 3 months of unowned revenue in LTM
  • Promotional lift assumptions applied inconsistently across schedules
What external evidence says
  • Retail scanner data corroborates the +9% velocity claim within 70bps
  • Hiring and review signal shows the club relationship active as of Jul-26
  • One competitor cut club pricing in Q2 — a margin watch item
  • No litigation, lien, or UCC red flags in the external sweep
